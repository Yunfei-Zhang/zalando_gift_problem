Zalando is searching for Applied Scientists who enjoy solving mathematical problems and are passionate about making a difference. If this speaks to you, we warmly invite you to submit your application and share your solution instead of a traditional cover letter.

We all have a busy life, so if you like such problems but do not have the time, we are also happy to consider your application! 

### The problem description

Sergey's birthday is approaching and Ahmad wants to find him the most expensive present. Ahmad’s backpack can only carry 40 liters. This is why he will need to make a decision on selecting the best items from the Zalando website.

Accidentally, because of a mistake, Oliver wiped the whole database table containing item volumes, the only information we have is the volume of packages that were delivered to our customers in the past. The machine measuring the volumes of the packages does not have a perfect precision, the error is following a normal distribution of parameters (mean=0, variance=2). Here is the dataset: [packages.json](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1pnzC9I5cTAfwUJ05SZs4qyjkmoz_hzTP/view?usp=drive_link)

Anna decided to support Ahmad with his quest and scraped the Zalando website to get the price of all items sold. Here is the dataset: [items.json](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1tMVLjnotR2RdZ_c3XIQZIN1l7TC6rSeD/view?usp=drive_link)

We would appreciate, if you could support Ahmad making the best gift choices for Sergey's birthday.

notes:

1.   Sum of all item volumes in the package in Liters
2.   Note that items never appear more than once in the same package
